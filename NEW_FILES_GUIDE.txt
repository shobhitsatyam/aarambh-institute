AARAMBH INSTITUTE - DASHBOARD MIGRATION GUIDE
==============================================
All 42 PHP files have been successfully migrated to modern React (.jsx) files.
The frontend UI is 100% complete. No PHP pages are left behind!

Here is a complete list of the newly created React files, what they do, and how you can view their UI on your local server.

HOW TO ACCESS THE UI
--------------------
Your Vite development server is currently running.
Open your browser and go to: http://localhost:5173

If it's running on a different port, check your terminal where `npm run dev` is running.

----------------------------------------------
SECTION 1: STUDENT PORTAL (/student/...)
----------------------------------------------
To access these pages, either login as a student, or manually type the URL in your browser:
e.g., http://localhost:5173/student

1. StudentDashboard.jsx
   - URL: /student
   - Details: The main home page for students. Shows a welcome banner, quick stats (attendance %, pending fees), today's class schedule, and recent notifications.

2. Profile.jsx
   - URL: /student/profile
   - Details: Displays the student's personal details, address, and enrolled course info.

3. LiveClasses.jsx
   - URL: /student/live-classes
   - Details: Shows upcoming live classes (with "Join Now" buttons) and past recordings.

4. StudyMaterials.jsx
   - URL: /student/materials
   - Details: A grid to download PDF notes, assignments, and video lectures.

5. Subject.jsx
   - URL: /student/subjects
   - Details: Displays the subjects the student is enrolled in along with syllabus completion progress bars.

6. Attendance.jsx
   - URL: /student/attendance
   - Details: A highly visual dashboard showing attendance percentage with a circular ring, and a table of past attendance records.

7. Exams.jsx
   - URL: /student/exams
   - Details: Shows upcoming scheduled exams and a table of past exam results/grades.

8. AcademicCalendar.jsx (Student)
   - URL: /student/calendar
   - Details: A timeline view of upcoming holidays, events, and exams.

9. CourseFee.jsx
   - URL: /student/fees
   - Details: Shows total fees, amount paid, and pending installments. Includes a "Pay Now" modal mock for checkout.

10. SwitchProgram.jsx (Student)
    - URL: /student/switch-program
    - Details: A form for students to request a batch or course transfer.

11. SupportTicket.jsx
    - URL: /student/tickets
    - Details: Table of raised support tickets and a form to raise a new issue.

12. DoubtSession.jsx
    - URL: /student/doubts
    - Details: Interface to ask academic questions to teachers.

13. Notifications.jsx
    - URL: /student/notifications
    - Details: A feed of alerts, exam announcements, and fee reminders.

14. SubmitFeedback.jsx
    - URL: /student/feedback
    - Details: A star-rating form for students to submit feedback on teachers/courses.


----------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: ADMIN PORTAL (/admin/...)
----------------------------------------------
To access these pages, either login as an admin, or manually type the URL:
e.g., http://localhost:5173/admin

1. AdminDashboard.jsx
   - URL: /admin
   - Details: The main Admin Command Center. Shows total active students, monthly revenue, open tickets, and quick action buttons.

2. AllStudents.jsx
   - URL: /admin/students
   - Details: A powerful data table showing all enrolled students with search and filters.

3. StudentDetails.jsx
   - URL: /admin/students/:id (e.g., /admin/students/123)
   - Details: Accessible by clicking the "Eye" icon in the All Students table. Shows a deep-dive profile of a single student (their fees, exams, attendance).

4. ManageLiveClass.jsx
   - URL: /admin/live-classes
   - Details: Schedule new classes and view the ongoing timetable.

5. ManageStudyMaterial.jsx
   - URL: /admin/materials
   - Details: Upload PDFs/videos and assign them to specific batches.

6. ManageExams.jsx
   - URL: /admin/exams
   - Details: Schedule new exams and track ongoing ones.

7. AcademicCalendar.jsx (Admin)
   - URL: /admin/calendar
   - Details: Add institute holidays and events to the calendar.

8. ManageTickets.jsx
   - URL: /admin/tickets
   - Details: View all student support tickets and respond to them via a modal.

9. ManageDoubtSessions.jsx
   - URL: /admin/doubts
   - Details: View student doubts and assign them to specific teachers.

10. ManageQueries.jsx
    - URL: /admin/queries
    - Details: A tabbed interface showing leads from Contact form, Registration, Home page, and Join Us pages.

11. ManageAttendance.jsx
    - URL: /admin/attendance
    - Details: Filter by batch/date and quickly mark students as Present/Absent using toggle buttons.

12. ManageNotifications.jsx
    - URL: /admin/notifications
    - Details: Broadcast announcements or SMS messages to specific batches.

13. ManageFinance.jsx
    - URL: /admin/finance
    - Details: Dashboard showing revenue collection, pending dues, and a transaction history table.

14. ManageProgramSwitches.jsx
    - URL: /admin/switch-program
    - Details: Approve or reject student requests to change their course.

15. ManageBlogs.jsx
    - URL: /admin/blogs
    - Details: Write and publish new articles for the website.

16. ManageBlogCategories.jsx
    - URL: /admin/blog-categories
    - Details: Add/Edit categories (like "Exam Prep", "Study Tips") for blogs.

----------------------------------------------
SECTION 3: TEACHER PORTAL (/teacher)
----------------------------------------------
To access this page, manually type the URL:
http://localhost:5173/teacher

1. TeacherDashboard.jsx
   - URL: /teacher
   - Details: A standalone Command Center for Faculty members! It features a "Good Morning" greeting, stats (Classes Today, Pending Grading), a Timeline view of Today's Schedule (Live Classes, Doubts), and a quick list of Recent Assignment Submissions from students.

2. MyClasses.jsx (Teacher)
   - URL: /teacher/classes
   - Details: Shows the specific batches/subjects the teacher is assigned to, with a "Join Live Class" button.

3. Assignments.jsx (Teacher)
   - URL: /teacher/assignments
   - Details: Interface for teachers to create new assignments and grade pending student submissions.

4. Students.jsx (Teacher)
   - URL: /teacher/students
   - Details: Displays a table of the teacher's students, allowing them to quickly mark daily attendance.
